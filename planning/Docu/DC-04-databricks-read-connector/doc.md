# Databricks Read Connector

## Subject

The read-only source in v1: how a Unity Catalog schema becomes a data product and its tables become datasets.

## Scope

**In:** OAuth M2M auth, the read-only capability manifest, change detection by checksum snapshot, reading schemas and tables, the source-to-neutral mapping, and the SQL-gated tag read over the Statement Execution API.

**Out:** Any write path (source connectors are read-only in v1), Delta shares and Marketplace listings, and lineage.
